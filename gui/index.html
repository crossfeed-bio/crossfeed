Read a grownet interaction network: focus by species, inspect the evidence behind each edge, download for Cytoscape or the pipeline.
How a network is derived
This viewer reads a network that is already derived; it does not derive. The settings live on the local page (grownet gui, Advanced settings) and on the command line (grownet derive --help), with the same defaults, and every network records the ones it was made with in meta.settings. By default: the area under the curve, compared as the mean log2 over replicate sets, after a no-growth check; strains keyed by NCBI taxon id; two-member co-cultures and drop-out communities; the same species-identifying technique on both sides; batch cultures only; an interaction absent when |log2 mean| is under one sd; the test the derivation ran, named in the network's meta.statistics, with Benjamini-Hochberg, reported and never deciding. The reasons are in docs/METHOD_NOTES.md.
